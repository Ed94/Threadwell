The true "killer app" for MR should be the OS / Home UX. You've complained about the friction of entering VR. If the first thing you see when donning a headset was your real environment, with some really good diagetic UI, it would become more approachable to mass audiences. Simple MR apps would serve as training wheels to more complex immersive apps. I imagine Apple R&D found something similar, since they dedicated a hardware dial to control the MR/VR blend.
