FOSS creates a bizarro software world where the 'higher values' they aspire to completely clash with a basic understanding of incentives.

COMPANY:
"So you mean we can just fork this repo, never have to deal with the maintainers, and use it to make money?"

FOSS world:
"Umm, honestly shocked and confused by COMPANY's behavior?? Shouldn't they be contributing to making the software better for EVERYONE?"

Meanwhile...

FOSS user:
"It would be great if this software did X"

FOSS maintainer:
"Feel free to open a PR that implements X! I can only do so much, but this is FOSS! We're all in this together!"

*PR stays open for years with no viable solution*
