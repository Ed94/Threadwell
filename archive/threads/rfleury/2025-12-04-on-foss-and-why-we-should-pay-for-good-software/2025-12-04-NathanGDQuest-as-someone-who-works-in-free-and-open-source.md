As someone who works in free and open source software, over time, I feel more and more in a similar way.

Many of us actively participating and promoting Free Software actually do it more in the spirit of your local Fab Lab or Makerspace.

Usage of free software is largely anonymous and silent, so you tend not to really realize the reach and impact it has until it's reached far and wide.
