Text files, while limiting and underpowered, feel like solid ground. In my view, a solid next step is a broad, general-purpose data format open standard (with a tooling suite)—like text—but with primitives for graphical and structural information.

**2/** **@rfleury** ^1740841420502835639

This marries the benefits of terminals, text files, text logs, and so on, with the opportunity for the dramatically improved UI/UX offered by graphical user interfaces.
