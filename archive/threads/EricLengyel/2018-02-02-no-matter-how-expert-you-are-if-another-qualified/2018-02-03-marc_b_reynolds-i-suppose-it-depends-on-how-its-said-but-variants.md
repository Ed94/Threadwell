I suppose it depends on how it's said but variants of "why don't/didn't you..X?" I might mean as "This is probably obvious but why dismiss X?"

**2/** **@tom_forsyth** ^959868589565124608

**@marc_b_reynolds** **@EricLengyel** **@joeludwig**

"Why don't you just X..." implies that the (skilled) person you are asking is too dumb to have thought of obvious idea X.

A better way to phrase the question is "what happens when X..." or "what problems does doing X cause?" Implies they tried/thought of X already.
