There's a neat property about the quadratic formula that most people don't seem to be aware of. The root given by the plus sign is always where the curve crosses the x axis with a positive derivative, and the root given by the minus sign always has a negative derivative.

![](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/GLKwwRlaQAAYTpv?format=png&name=orig)

**2/** **@EricLengyel** ^1779680640126030137

This is obvious if you plug the roots into df/dx, and it's true regardless of the sign of a, which determines whether the parabola opens upward or downward. This means you never need to actually calculate the derivative at a root if you want to know which way the curve is going.
