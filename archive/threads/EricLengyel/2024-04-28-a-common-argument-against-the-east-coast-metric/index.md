A common argument against the east coast metric signature (−+++) is that timelike magnitudes are negative. This problem evaporates in 5D projective space-time exterior algebra because the weight component of a homogeneous magnitude cancels the minus sign. Projected magnitudes are always positive.

Furthermore, the metric exomorphism G always contains both the east coast and west coast metrics g within the full 32×32 tensor. Below, the green box contains the 5×5 projective metric for vectors with the east coast signature (−+++0), and its west coast counterpart appears in the purple box for trivectors.
