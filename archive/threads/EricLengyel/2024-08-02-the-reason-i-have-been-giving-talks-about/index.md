I developed antiproducts as part of my own research, starting around 2010, and PGA Illuminated is currently the only text that provides a complete treatment. The term “antivector” is just an alternate term for “pseudovector” or “(n-1)-vector” that is more consistent with the rest of the terminology used in the complete algebra.
