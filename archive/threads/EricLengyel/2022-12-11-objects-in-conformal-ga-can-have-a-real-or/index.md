**1/** **@EricLengyel** ^1601902349601058816

Objects in conformal GA can have a real or imaginary radius. I need a term that describes the property of being real or imaginary without regard for size.
One could ask "What is the _____ of X?", and the answer would be either "real" or "imaginary". What should go in the blank?

**2/** **@EricLengyel** ^1601902893791006720

We usually only see the square of the radius, which is positive in the real case and negative in the imaginary case. A fitting term could reflect this positive or negative property instead of the realness or imaginariness of the radius.

**3/** **@fatlimey** ^1602124968133038081

**@EricLengyel**

Make up your own terminology, e.g."Is the value a 'forward' or 'backward' vector?". Implying general directionality without explicitly talking about sign or basis.

**4/** **@EricLengyel** ^1602170950971105280

**@fatlimey**

The terms "real" and "imaginary" are already established for the value of the property, but the property itself doesn't have a convenient name. I'm seeing more and more cases when I want to talk about this property by name without specifying an exact value. 1/2

**5/** **@EricLengyel** ^1602171519832317952

**@fatlimey**

For example,

• An object's container has the same ____ as the object itself.
• The dual of an object has the opposite ____.
• This function flips the object's ____.

In each of these, ____ would be the name of the property that can have the value "real" or "imaginary".
