Being able to edit many things at once is a sorely missing feature in most UIs.

Say I have a form on a webpage. Why can't I copy/paste the entire form? Why can I only edit text in a single field at a time?

**2/** **@rfleury** ^1696338656284615101

**@Hasen_Judi**

Because the text part is standardized as a medium, and the form part is not - there is not enough structure in the underlying substrate.

**3/** **@hasen_95dx** ^1696344406436290936

**@rfleury**

It's possible to just treat it as a list of text fields with no other structure.

Spreadsheet programs do something like this.
