I've gone from agreeing with the camp of "this will never produce anything truly good" to "you can actually use this incrementally in your old workflow". The productivity gains are nowhere near as fast as some people might make you believe, though.

I understand the naysayers nowadays trying to protect their sourcecode. At the same time, implying that if you are someone who produces "good things" that are above the AI-slop you *never have any work on the computer that doesnt need verification* is also insane. you can just use AI to do supplementary throwaway work where you benefit from the results and still use those in your verifiable process. Game developers have always been "prototyping" before making the real thing, well, you can now prototype 10x faster, which is at least something.
