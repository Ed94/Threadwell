Good breakdown. In addition to retroactive verification, non-AI workflows have the machine verify the human output, while AI workflows have humans verify the machine output. 

This added level of indirection is what creates a tradeoff between speed and control. You get faster code you can't control AND that you have to verify. I'm not so thrilled with this tradeoff, it feels antithetical to engineering where precision and control have always been central.
