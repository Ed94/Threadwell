This is exactly what I am trying to explain to people, they have to read code, they have to create intent, they have to link this all together to make it Coherent.

I also realized that our reality itself is "code", you can't ever stop "reading" it or "understanding" it. Agents can produce patterns for you, but they can't produce understanding in your head. You can't outsource understanding.

When you create a software by hand, you are literally manipulating reality, rearranging electrons in the RAM/CPU when you're typing and so how come you shouldn't understand what you are doing?

We must stop people that are telling us otherwise.
