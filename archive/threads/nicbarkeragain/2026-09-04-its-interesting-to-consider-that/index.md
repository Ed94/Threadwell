It’s interesting to consider that
a) the effort required to do something by hand acts as selection pressure on the ideas themselves i.e only promising ideas are pursued
b) practising that selection process makes you better at it, therefore not practising (e.g LLM use) erodes it

**3/** **@kssreeram** ^2095808412110258593

I do agree with the sentiment.

If I may play devil’s advocate in an attempt to refine the argument…

Can’t the same argument be made against any effort reduction technology? For instance Cars? Cars reduce effort in moving from one place to another. Does using cars cause the same kind of harm?

It doesn’t. Because cars let us retain control far better than LLMs do in programming.

Perhaps, the problem doesn’t lie with a reduction in effort, but with loss of control?
