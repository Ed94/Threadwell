If you want a good topology book, look at Brown's Topology and Groupoids. Schuller's geometric anatomy of physics videos introduces dynkin diagrams, and books by Helgason, Serre, and Sepanski each use them. Lenia are a meme, but the non meme version is solitons/monopoles in nonlinear PDE theory. I have more like this if you are interested.
