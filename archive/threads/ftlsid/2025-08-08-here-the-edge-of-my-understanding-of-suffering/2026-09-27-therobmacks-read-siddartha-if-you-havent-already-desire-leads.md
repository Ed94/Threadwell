read Siddartha if you haven't already. 

desire leads to suffering, but suffering is not the achievement either. The key is the middle way.

the middle way is mapped through Noble Eightfold Path.   Correct thoughts, correct action, correct speech, effort, mindfulness, efforts, etc.

you are suffering because of your desires in this "illusion". 

your existence is to just be. 

all things in harmony simply exist. we are misled by *pulling* things towards us that we desire, and *pushing* things away from us that we do not desire. 

correct thoughts are to not have fear of the things we do not want to happen and vice versa, seeking fulfillment, or feelings of scarcity of the things you believe you do not have.

when you are able to exist without any desire for any of it, including the desire to no longer suffer, then you will be free. 

by simply accepting everything as is during the existence, your suffering will end.
