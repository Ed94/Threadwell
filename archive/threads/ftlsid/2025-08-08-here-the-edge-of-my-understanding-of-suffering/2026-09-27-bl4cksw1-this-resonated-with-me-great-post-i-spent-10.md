This resonated with me. Great post.

I spent 10+ years, up to ~February this year, operating from scripts I wrote a lot time ago. Scripts that I probably didn’t even author myself, but were almost directly downstream of memories, experiences when I was a child and so on. This is the cruel irony - you think it is what you want for yourself, AND the ability to have a plan and ‘discipline’ is greatly rewarded in our society.

This year I went through somewhat of a transformation much like you describe, whereby, I started to observe all of the behaviours, thoughts that were driving my behaviour. Simply, I started to say yes to what I wanted, in the moment, and no to the things I didn’t want. By accident, mostly, I felt myself becoming happier, and more free as these shackles dropped off. Much like you describe, too, I caught myself many times finding deep and profound joy in the things I used to miss. I no longer had to practice gratitude - I was simply grateful.

I am still working towards goals, but I have dialled it back to what feels like a delicate point of balance. I do this on my terms, and that means when I sit down to focus I am so locked in, in a way that I just could never reach before. I don’t even realise, but hours will go by and I will notice I haven’t been distracted once.
