I intuitively feel that your words resonate deeply with my psyche (mainly the unconscious), although I lack the vocabulary to articulate them so granularly.

I don't inherently subscribe to mysticism / superstitions, but I always found Jung's concept of Synchronicity to be an anomaly.  Just yesterday, I stumbled across a Japanese movie called "Perfect Days".

Most of the movie, we follow the ordinary routine of a public toilet cleaner in Tokyo. 

If your dopamine receptors are overstimulated, you'll find the storyline dull. But if you really sit with the character and look at his eyes as he gazes in wonderment at a gentle sun ray caressing the foliage that rustles in the spring breeze, you'll start to grasp what the expression "There are cathedrals everywhere for those with the eyes to see" means.

Only a day after seeing that movie, I come across your insightful (and quite psychedelic) post AFTER days of ravaging my own mind, struggling to find words for new sensations emerging from my unconscious.
(hence why I can't help but refer to this as synchronicity)

Perhaps what we're all trying to achieve here is unknotting this amalgame  which we call "subjective self". 

Paradoxically, it's what Alan Watts referred to as "the self needing the change is the very self who does the change" i.e the tongue can't taste itself, the eye can't see itself. In the attempt to undo the knots, you became tangled in them. 

Here, one can wonder if the best course of action is to follow in the footsteps of Alexander the Great and unsheathe his sword before the Gordian knot.
