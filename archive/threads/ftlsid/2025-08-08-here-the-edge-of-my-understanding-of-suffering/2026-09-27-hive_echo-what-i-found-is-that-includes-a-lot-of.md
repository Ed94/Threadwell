What I found is that ( includes a lot of presumptions ) there is no ultimate enlightenment or attainment as far as I could tell by observing/listening to others who are by now supposed to have achieved it. No permanent solution 

Also I find realizing “no self” etc not the best solution as I think self is just as natural construct of the mind as is awareness ( unless there is a universal awareness field or something which i would evaluate at low probability, tho I ultimately don’t know )

Reasons are simple IMO, human organism just as all organisms are driven by drives that are designed in a world full of physical and resource restrictions. Hence the primary drives just push with great power and force enabling high persistence. Hence in a world abundant in the affordances which allows one to act naturally and without any or little self or top down restrictions lead to obesity, addictions, “I can’t get no satisfaction” type of outcomes 

In this sense balancing naturally arising from inside with provided from outside is a delicate Pareto frontier ( no exact stable solutions either as the frontier itself evolves )

All in all, conclusion I have reached is this:

Human mind/brain is capable of wiring itself to accommodate all sorts of strange configurations. And it seems limitless in that sense 

So a continually evolving solution appears to me is to utilize all available methods - meditation, mindfulness, IFS,  meta cognition, modern techniques etc - to continually aim for re-wiring for Pareto optimal configurations without end. This is why first aiming for a flexible and easily  re-wirable state is desirable IMO

So in a nutshell, aiming to rewire to allow further rewiring cheaper and faster then continually rewiring towards an ever changing Pareto optimal configurations without any end. But there could be temporary blissful states along the way for sure 

I wish there was an ultimate achievement and maybe there is. But I am not convinced but you never know and if I attain it, I will update :)
