I’ve taken to using a simple interview question with folks. It’s surprising how much people seem to struggle with answering it.

Tell me how you would efficiently sum a large array of numbers on a GPU into a single accumulated value.

Don’t need to see code, just explain it conceptually in relation to the hardware.
