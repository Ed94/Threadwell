I still buy loads of programming books. If I'm approaching a new subject I'll open up an AI and ask 'recommend me a good book covering <blah>'... 

AI's have their place, and they can present info in a very concise way... but they have no character.. they have no stories to tell... they're not telling you their experience, they are telling you distilled information.

They're not handing down wisdom.

Give me a good book any day, written by someone who has survived the battle.
